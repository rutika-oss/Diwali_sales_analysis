# 🎇 Diwali Sales Analysis

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Diwali sales data to uncover customer purchasing behavior, sales trends, and business opportunities. Using Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn, the analysis provides actionable insights to help businesses improve marketing strategies and increase sales during festive seasons.

---

## 🎯 Objective

- Analyze customer demographics and purchasing patterns.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Discover high-value customer segments.
- Generate business insights to improve festive season sales.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## 📊 Key Analysis Performed

- Data Cleaning and Preprocessing
- Gender-wise Purchase Analysis
- Age Group Analysis
- State-wise Sales Performance
- Marital Status Analysis
- Occupation-based Purchase Trends
- Product Category Analysis
- Top Selling Products Identification

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Female customers contributed more to overall sales than male customers.
- Customers aged 26–35 years made the highest number of purchases.
- Married customers showed higher purchasing power.
- Most orders came from states such as Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka.
- IT, Healthcare, and Aviation professionals were among the highest spending customer groups.
- Food, Clothing, and Electronics were the most purchased product categories.

---

## 💼 Business Impact

- Helps businesses identify target customer segments.
- Supports data-driven marketing and promotional campaigns.
- Enables better inventory planning during festive seasons.
- Improves customer engagement and sales performance.
